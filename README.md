## What the video covers

- **Length:** 10 minutes 1 second; **1920 × 1080**, 30 fps.
- Seven embedded chapters and selectable English captions.
- Real Nana screenshots with animated pans and zooms, plus Manim diagrams.
  The walkthrough is an edited screenshot presentation rather than a continuous screen recording.
- Edited Google Notebook synthetic narration. Captions and transcript are
  automatic and lightly corrected; recognition errors may remain.
- A fictional workshop brief demonstrates local summarisation, saving a
  workspace note, a basic DOCX download and a separate reviewed tool action.

## Snapshot and limitations

Produced **21 October 2026**, using Nana source revision
[`8b44cf6`](https://github.com/sujugithub/nana/commit/8b44cf6a44e53a7d5109d782fa52da112446cb33);
published **27 October 2026**. Verified features and unfinished work are
distinguished throughout the film. Production readiness and routing savings
remain to be established.

Since filming, Settings gained three router choices, and real Arch-Router
decisions were verified locally on 27 October. The film's description of
Arch-Router as a spike reflects the recording date. Current-pair routing
quality, latency and cost comparisons remain outstanding. See the
[integration notes](https://github.com/sujugithub/nana/blob/main/docs/ARCH_ROUTER_NOTICE.md)
for the implementation and dated evidence.
